01 / Project Overview

The Research Challenge

Breast cancer genomics faces a critical dimensionality problem — discovering meaningful biomarkers from an ocean of genetic noise.

PROBLEM

High Dimensionality

Genomic datasets contain tens of thousands of features — far exceeding the number of patient samples available.

CHALLENGE

p ≫ n Problem

16,519 genes for only 923 patients creates severe overfitting risk — classical methods struggle here.

GOAL

Clinical Biomarkers

Identify a compact, clinically relevant set of genes that reliably distinguishes cancer from normal tissue.

INNOVATION

QAOA Optimization

Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm formulates biomarker selection as a combinatorial QUBO problem.

06 / Quantum Optimization

QAOA Formulation

The Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm encodes biomarker selection as a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) problem, exploiting quantum superposition to explore exponentially large solution spaces.

α
FEATURE RELEVANCE
Reward for selecting genes with high mutual information relative to the cancer label
∑ᵢ αᵢ · I(Gᵢ ; Y) · xᵢ
β
REDUNDANCY PENALTY
Penalize co-selection of highly correlated genes to maximize information diversity
− β · ∑ᵢ≠ⱼ C(Gᵢ,Gⱼ) · xᵢxⱼ
γ
SPARSITY PENALTY
Enforce a compact feature subset — target selection of exactly k biomarker genes
− γ · (∑ᵢ xᵢ − k)²
QUBO OBJECTIVE FUNCTION
H(x) = −α · ∑ᵢ MI(Gᵢ) · xᵢ
+β · ∑ᵢ<ⱼ Corr(Gᵢ,Gⱼ) · xᵢ · xⱼ
+γ · (∑ᵢ xᵢ − k)²
where xᵢ ∈ {0,1} denotes gene selection, k=10 is the target subset size, and the optimization is performed via QAOA with p=2 layers.

11 / Future Work

Research Roadmap

Larger Quantum Hardware
Near-term

Scaling QAOA to real quantum processors (IBM Quantum, IonQ) with 50–100+ qubits enables larger candidate gene subsets and more complex QUBO formulations.

Multi-Omics Integration
Mid-term

Extending the pipeline to incorporate RNA-seq expression, copy number variation, and methylation data for a holistic multi-modal biomarker selection framework.

Advanced QUBO Formulations
Research

Incorporating pathway-level constraints, protein-protein interaction networks, and biologically-informed penalty terms directly into the QUBO objective.

Deep Learning Integration
Long-term

Replacing logistic regression with graph neural networks or transformers operating on quantum-selected gene interaction graphs for improved prediction.

External Validation Cohorts
Clinical

Validating the 10 QAOA-selected biomarkers in independent cohorts (METABRIC, CBCGA) and prospective clinical settings to confirm translational relevance.
